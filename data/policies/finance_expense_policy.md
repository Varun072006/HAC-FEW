# Enterprise Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy
Document ID: POL-FIN-002
Department: Finance
Access Role: finance_rep, manager, employee
Version: 3.0

## Section 1: Expense Tiers and Approval Thresholds
Reimbursement requests are categorized into three financial tiers:
1. Low Value (< $100.00 USD): Automatically approved if submitted with a valid itemized digital receipt.
2. Moderate Value ($100.00 to $1,000.00 USD): Requires automated Finance Agent compliance check plus one designated Line Manager approval.
3. High Value (> $1,000.00 USD): Requires Finance Agent policy validation AND dual human authorization (Department Director + Finance Controller).

## Section 2: Non-Reimbursable Categories
The following expenditures are strictly non-reimbursable under enterprise corporate guidelines:
- Personal alcohol consumption or bar tabs exceeding 20% of dinner bill.
- Traffic or parking citations incurred during company travel.
- First-class or business-class flights for domestic routes under 6 hours flight time.

## Section 3: Receipt and Submission Window
All claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the transaction date.
Any expense lacking a readable itemized receipt will be rejected automatically by the Finance Agent.
