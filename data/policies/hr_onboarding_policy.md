# Enterprise HR Onboarding & Leave Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-001
Department: HR
Access Role: hr_rep, manager, employee
Version: 2.1

## Section 1: New Hire Onboarding Procedure
All newly hired personnel must be registered into the HRMS database within 24 hours of offer acceptance. 
The HR representative must record: full legal name, work email address, department code, and primary job title.
Upon HRMS record creation, the employee receives an onboarding status flag `PENDING_PROVISIONING`.

## Section 2: Account and Equipment Request Triggers
Immediately after HRMS employee record generation:
1. IT provisioning must be notified to issue a primary email account (`@company.test`) and assign hardware profile (Standard Laptop for Engineering/Product, Thin-client for Operations).
2. Finance must be notified to configure payroll compensation records and default expense tier.

## Section 3: Leave Entitlements
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid annual leave per calendar month (total 21 days annually).
Probationary employees (first 90 days) may take up to 3 emergency sick days but cannot take annual vacation days without prior written manager approval.
